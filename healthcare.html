The Consent & Care Gap

Thousands Wouldn’t is an independent research project, run by people with lived experience of PMDD, not a medical professional, clinic, or charity. Nothing on this site is medical advice. If you need support with your mental or physical health, we would urge you to speak to a medical professional.

Some of what’s shared here reflects difficult experiences. Please take care of yourself first and foremost, and take your time with anything you read or answer. Your answers to any of our forms are pseudonymous — we don’t collect your name or any contact details.

  • Call Samaritans, free, day or night: 116 123.
  • Text SHOUT to 85258 for free, confidential, 24/7 text support.
  • NHS 111, option 2, helps you find urgent local mental health support.
